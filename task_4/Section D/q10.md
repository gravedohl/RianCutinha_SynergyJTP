## Question 10
Do MidSem Averages Hide Inconsistency?
Group by subject and compute the mean, standard deviation, minimum and maximum marks in one agg call. Rank the subjects by mean, then by standard deviation. Interpret: two subjects have almost the same average. Which one would a teacher need to investigate further and why?

## Aim
To use groupby and agg to compare subjects and see how averages can hide inconsistency

## Code

```
import pandas as pd

marks = pd.DataFrame({
    'subject': ['Maths']*4 + ['Physics']*4 + ['Pps']*4,
    'marks': [72, 74, 70, 73, 60, 95, 50, 85, 80, 78, 82, 84]})
g = marks.groupby('subject')['marks'].agg(['mean', 'std', 'min', 'max']).round(2)
print(g)
print(g.sort_values('mean', ascending=False))
print(g.sort_values('std', ascending=False))
```

## Explanation
agg(['mean', 'std', 'min', 'max']) gives all four stats in one call  
ranking by mean gives pps, physics, maths  
ranking by std gives physics, pps, maths  
maths and physics have almost the same average  
but physics has std 21.02 and marks from 50 to 95 while maths is just 70 to 74  
so a teacher should investigate physics, the average hides that students are doing very differently
