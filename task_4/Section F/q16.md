## Question 16
Manipal Weather Swings. 
Manipal’s weather is very unpredictable . weather station wants to know when temperatures swung the most. Plot both series on one line chart with different markers and line styles, a legend, a grid and labelled axes (with units). Use NumPy to find the day with the largest daily range (high − low). Interpret: does the day with the highest temperature also have the largest swing?

## Aim
To plot daily high and low temperatures on one chart and find the day with the biggest temperature swing

## Code

```
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

days = np.arange(1, 11)
high = np.array([31, 32, 30, 29, 33, 35, 34, 28, 27, 30])
low = np.array([24, 25, 23, 24, 25, 26, 24, 22, 22, 23])

plt.plot(days, high, marker='o', linestyle='-', color='red', label='High')
plt.plot(days, low, marker='s', linestyle='--', color='blue', label='Low')
plt.xlabel('Day')
plt.ylabel('Temperature (°C)')
plt.title('Manipal Daily High and Low Temperatures')
plt.legend()
plt.grid(True)
plt.show()

diff = high - low
print(diff)
print(days[diff.argmax()], diff.max())
print(days[high.argmax()], high.max())
```

## Output

```
[ 7  7  7  5  8  9 10  6  5  7]
7 10
6 35

```

## Explanation
high uses a solid line with circle markers and low uses a dashed line with square markers 
legend, grid and axis labels are added  
high - low gives the daily range for every day at once  
argmax gives the position of the largest range, which is day 7 with a 10 °C swing  
the highest temperature was on day 6  
so no, the hottest day is not the day with the biggest swing
